What it does

Everything a sideline coach needs, nothing they don't

Real-time stat tracking

Points, rebounds, assists, steals, blocks, turnovers, and fouls with a single tap. Field goals, threes, and free throws tracked separately for accurate shooting splits.

Live game timelineNEW

See exactly when each player was on the court and when they scored, laid out against a running team +/- line — so you can read momentum against the lineup that produced it.

Substitutions & rotation fairness

Swap players with a purpose-built substitution screen. A rotation timer helps keep playing time fair, with an optional auto-pause during subs.

Rosters that remember

Unlimited teams and roster size, player photos for fast on-court identification, and full career stat history preserved even after a player retires.

Box scores & history

Full box scores and period-by-period breakdowns for every past game, with CSV export for deeper analysis in your spreadsheet of choice.

Seasons & leaderboards

Group games into seasons, track season-specific stats, and check Top 5 leaderboards for points, rebounds, assists, steals, and minutes played.
